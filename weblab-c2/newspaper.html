Perplexity.in redirecting users to Google Gemini

Perplexity missed securing India's country-specific domain (.in), leading an unrelated third party to purchase it and establish an unauthorized redirect that sends users directly to its competitor, Google Gemini. While there is no evidence that Google bought the domain, this lapse reflects Perplexity's broader global strategy of operating strictly through its primary Perplexity.ai site rather than buying region-specific extensions like .uk or .fr. Despite this domain blunder, the San Francisco-based AI startup is aggressively scaling its presence in India. The company recently rolled out its Comet AI browser to Pro users and partnered with telecom giant Airtel to offer Perplexity Pro subscriptions to its customers across the country.
