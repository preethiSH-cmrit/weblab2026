AI /ML projects

Posted OnJuly 12 2026
An AI/ML student behavior project focuses on leveraging machine learning algorithms to analyze, predict, and understand various educational and behavioral patterns of students. By processing diverse datasets—such as online learning management system (LMS) logs, class attendance, engagement levels, and assessment scores—the project can identify students at risk of academic failure, detect signs of burnout, or predict dropout rates early. Key techniques include using classification algorithms to predict academic outcomes, clustering (like K-Means) to segment students based on their unique learning styles, and Natural Language Processing (NLP) to analyze sentiment from student feedback.

My travels

Nandi Hills

Posted OnSept 8 2026
Nandi Hills, also known as Nandidurga, is an ancient hill fortress located roughly 60 kilometres north of Bengaluru, rising 1,478 meters above sea level. Famously celebrated for its breathtaking, panoramic sunrise views over a blanket of low-floating clouds, it serves as the ultimate weekend morning getaway for city dwellers.

Mysore Palace

Posted OnJuly 12 2026
Mysore Palace, also known as the Amba Vilas Palace, is a breathtaking royal residence located in the heart of Mysuru, Karnataka, serving as the official seat of the historic Wadiyar dynasty. Commissioned by Maharaja Krishnaraja Wodeyar IV and designed by renowned British architect Henry Irwin, this three-story granite structure was completed in 1912 after the original wooden palace was tragically destroyed by fire.
