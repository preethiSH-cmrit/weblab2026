They made the most tackles in a Test match

Ian Payten
The Wallabies won’t be banking on Wales suffering from a helpful hangover in Cardiff this week, despite the potential for the Welsh to be physically and emotionally drained after their historic victory over New Zealand. Emotional Wales players were in tears on Sunday morning and did a lap of honour at Millennium Stadium after producing one of the greatest upsets in rugby history, beating the All Blacks for the first time in 73 years. The Welsh tackled themselves to exhaustion as the Kiwis pounded their line for most of the second-half, making a record 346 tackles, and with lock Daffyd James equalling Thierry Dusatoir’s individual record of 38 tackles, and seven players made over 20 tackles.
